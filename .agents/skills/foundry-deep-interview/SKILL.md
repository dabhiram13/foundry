# Deep Interview

Read `docs/WORKFLOW.md` first. Ask only questions whose answers change scope, behavior, or risk. Maintain a compact decision record, resolve contradictions, and turn the result into a bounded spec rather than an open-ended transcript. Use `scripts/marketcraft.py` for lifecycle state; do not handcraft state or receipts.
