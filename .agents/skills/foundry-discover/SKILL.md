# Discover

Read docs/WORKFLOW.md and docs/WORKFLOW-CONTRACTS.md. Inspect the existing brief,
evidence, assumptions, and request depth. Research only uncertainties that could
change the next decision. Use available authorized tools and dated sources;
record counterevidence, segment, evidence type, and limitations. Competitor pricing
pages are offers, not validated demand; synthetic personas are not customers.

Connect evidence and explicit assumptions to switching, pricing/value metric,
channel, and unit-economics hypotheses, then a bounded product brief and observable
criteria. Preserve proposals as proposals. Use the documented record interface
for authorized record writes. The owner decides consequential unresolved choices;
do not force blanket confirmation gates or exhaustive research for small changes.
Research does not authorize implementation, external effects, or skill imports.
