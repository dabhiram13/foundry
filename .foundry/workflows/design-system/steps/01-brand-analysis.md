# Step 1: Product and brand direction

Read AGENTS.md, docs/WORKFLOW.md, the adopted product brief, and the applicable
artifact contracts before acting. Follow the actual stack, assigned write paths,
and existing authority. These steps are options for the current scope, not a
mandatory full redesign. The adopted brief overrides generic visual recipes.

Inspect the relevant customer evidence, product brief, flows, prototype, existing
UI, and brand decisions. Identify the primary task, hierarchy, content density,
voice, device context, and desired character. Distinguish owner decisions from
proposals and assumptions; synthetic personas do not count as customer evidence.

Record a concise direction with examples and exclusions. Preserve a strong
existing direction rather than proposing visual variants automatically. Ask only
about consequential unresolved choices. Hand off the adopted direction and its
criterion/artifact references; do not select a hue from a universal mood formula.
