# Foundry design

The owner-selected visual direction is a dark, Superset-inspired developer tool:
near-black canvas, quiet charcoal panels, bright rectangular primary controls,
orange selection details, Inter typography and an original block-letter mark.

The maintained component, responsive and interaction contract is in
[docs/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md](docs/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md). That document is the design
reference for implementation; do not restore the retired Soft studio palette.
